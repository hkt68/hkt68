<analysis>
The trajectory documents the development of a full-stack application for a small business owner. The project started with the AI engineer exploring a boilerplate React/FastAPI/MongoDB codebase. The user, communicating in Turkish, initially requested a general application for stock, sales, and backup management.

The AI engineer first built a comprehensive inventory management system with a standard dashboard UI. Following user feedback, this was refined into a Point-of-Sale (POS) system with barcode scanning, a sales cart, and change calculation features.

Subsequent user requests led to further iterative development. Key features added include:
1.  An autocomplete search function for products on the POS screen.
2.  A quick add modal to create new products directly from the POS screen if a scanned barcode is not found.
3.  A complete Customer Relationship Management (CRM) module for cari hesap (current accounts/accounts receivable). This included creating, viewing, and managing customers.
4.  Integration of the CRM with the POS system to allow for veresiye (credit) sales, linking transactions to specific customers and tracking their debt.

The most recent work involves enhancing the CRM module. The user requested the ability to see a customer's purchase history, manually add debt or record payments, and print a summary of their account. The AI engineer was in the process of implementing these features in a detailed customer account modal when the trajectory ended.
</analysis>

<product_requirements>
The user, owner of a small business named Elite Medya Bilişim that sells stationery and office supplies, requires a web-based application to manage their operations. The core need is a Point-of-Sale (POS) system integrated with inventory and customer account management.

**Key Functional Requirements:**
1.  **Inventory Management:** Add, view, and track product stock levels.
2.  **POS System:**
    *   A primary sales screen for processing transactions.
    *   Ability to add products to a cart via barcode scanning or an autocomplete product search.
    *   If a scanned barcode is new, a prompt to quickly add the product to the inventory.
    *   Calculation of total price and change due.
3.  **Customer Account Management (Cari Hesap):**
    *   Create and manage a database of customers.
    *   Process veresiye (credit) sales, assigning the debt to a customer's account.
    *   In a dedicated customer view, display a detailed transaction history (what they bought, when).
    *   Allow for manual entry of debts or recording of payments received against their balance.
    *   Provide an option to print a summary/statement of the customer's account.

**Ultimate Goal:**
The system should be embeddable or integrable into the user's existing Wix website, , with a public-facing sales/product page and a private admin panel for management.
</product_requirements>
